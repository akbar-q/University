# LO4 Professional Discussion — Folder

Purpose
- Store LO4 professional discussion (oral exam) supporting documents and notes.
- LO4 is focused on analysing alternative strategies for using other types of programmable control devices in industrial applications.

Assessment context (Task 4)
- Task 4: Alternative Automation Strategies - Professional Discussion
- Participate in a professional oral discussion (live and recorded) critically evaluating alternatives to PLCs for similar automation tasks.

What to include here
- Student PD preparation notes (bullet answers, comparison tables, diagrams)
- Tutor question bank and probing follow-ups
- PD sample Q&A (revision)
- Assessor notes and outcome summary (post-discussion)
- Any supporting evidence shown during the discussion (e.g., comparison matrices, risk tables)

Organisation suggestion
- PD Test Question List LO4.md (tutor-led question bank)
- PD Sample QnA LO4.md (student revision)
- PD_notes_<StudentName>.md (post-discussion notes)

Mapping to LO4 assessment criteria (exact)
- LO4.P7 Review the different types of programmable control devices available.
- LO4.P8 Examine an industrial application to determine the required characteristics of a control device.
- LO4.M6 Review the problems faced by using alternative devices in an industrial environment.
- LO4.D4 Critically evaluate the selection of an alternative programmable device in a given application.

Vocational scenario anchor
- MedPack Solutions Ltd. pill counting and packaging station (start/reset/e-stop, pill sensor, actuator, buzzer; safety and reliability expectations).
