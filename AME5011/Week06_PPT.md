# Week 6 - Top Engagement, Release and Repeatability

---

## Slide 1: Top Engagement Is a Timed Mission Step

- Contact the barrier with the top datum surface.
- Red light and buzzer must operate during engagement.
- Target 10-15 s for the highest stated engagement score; do not exceed 15 s.
- Then leave the barrier and continue the descent.

---

## Slide 2: The Chain Release Window

- Release only after passing the intermediate target and before the first/start target.
- Use position sensing or a robust mechanical sequence based on verified geometry.
- Test release repeatedly at the real descent speed, not only by hand.

---

## Slide 3: Design for Reliability

- Identify failure modes: missed barrier, false trigger, wheel slip, jam, clip failure, battery sag and uncontrolled descent.
- Design one failure at a time into a controlled test.
- Prefer simple mechanisms with observable states.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - Mechanism Development

- Bench-test top detection/dwell response.
- Test clip release in the marked rig zone using video or a repeatable position record.
- Update an FMEA-style risk register and make the highest-value improvement.

---

## Slide 5: Week 6 Evidence

- Top engagement timing results.
- Release location results over repeated tests.
- Updated mechanism drawing, risk register and iteration record.
