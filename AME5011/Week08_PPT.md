# Week 8 - Communicate, Review and Compete

---

## Slide 1: Present the Design Story

- Challenge and measurable requirements.
- Selected concept and why it won.
- Key engineering calculations and test evidence.
- Cost, mass, safety and honest limitations.

---

## Slide 2: Competition Presentation Requirements

- Five minutes plus questions.
- Explain the final device, design development, expected/calculated performance and cost.
- Be ready to defend component, mechanism and test decisions with evidence.

---

## Slide 3: Poster Requirements

- A1 portrait format with university and IMechE logos plus team names.
- Clear device diagram/CAD and concise technical description.
- Engineering science, BoM cost and mass shown graphically and accurately.
- Use readable units, decimals and checked spelling.

---

## Slide 4: Peer Review and Judge Questions

- Explain design principles, simplicity, robustness, manufacturing quality and appearance.
- Observe competing devices without handling them.
- Answer questions directly: requirement, evidence, decision, trade-off.

---

## Slide 5: Final Submission Checklist

- Design dossier, CAD, code and test records.
- Final BoM, mass and cost statement.
- A1 poster and five-minute deck.
- Individual contribution reflection and safety documentation.
