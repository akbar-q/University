# Week 2 - Concepts, Pipe Contact and Selection

---

## Slide 1: Ways to Climb a Pipe

- Opposed driven rollers with compliant idlers.
- Belt or track wrapped around the pipe.
- Screw, cable or inchworm mechanisms.
- Internal and external architectures respond differently to the annual brief.

---

## Slide 2: Contact Mechanics Matters

- Required tangential force must remain below available friction: $F_{required} \leq \mu N$.
- The pipe surface, roller material, preload and alignment determine $\mu$ and $N$.
- Excess preload can waste power, deform parts or create a jam.

---

## Slide 3: A Defensible Concept Selection

- Generate distinct concepts before judging them.
- Use weighted criteria: compliance, traction, load capacity, manufacturability, cost, mass, safety, controllability and repairability.
- Score uncertainty honestly and list tests that would change the decision.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - Sketch, Model and Compare

- Create at least three concepts with a clear pipe-contact arrangement.
- Build quick traction/contact samples on a 22 mm pipe offcut.
- Complete a weighted matrix and select one concept for detailed development.

---

## Slide 5: Week 2 Evidence

- Concept sketches or CAD layouts.
- Selection matrix with weights and reasons.
- A test plan for the highest-risk assumption.
