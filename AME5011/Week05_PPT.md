# Week 5 - Manufacture and Integrate the Vehicle

---

## Slide 1: Build for Alignment and Repair

- Bearings, roller axes and the pipe centreline must remain aligned under load.
- Use accessible fasteners, positive cable retention and protected moving parts.
- Design adjustment features deliberately; avoid loose parts as a substitute for tolerance design.

---

## Slide 2: Control the Competition Envelope

- Model the 400 mm diameter by 300 mm length envelope in CAD.
- Identify the top datum surface used for barrier/start measurements.
- The automatic clip may protrude; other features must remain compliant throughout the run.

---

## Slide 3: Integration Test Sequence

1. Electrical inspection and unloaded bench motor test.
2. Static hold and pipe-contact inspection.
3. Short unladen climb/descent.
4. Loaded motion with controlled stop condition.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - First Integrated Build

- Manufacture and assemble the selected concept.
- Weigh and measure the vehicle; update CAD and BoM.
- Perform short, supervised rig tests and log faults before changing the design.

---

## Slide 5: Week 5 Evidence

- Updated assembly CAD and manufacturing record.
- Envelope/mass check.
- Integration test log with revisions linked to observed behaviour.
