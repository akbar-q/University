# Week 3 - Changing Load, Traction, Torque and Speed

---

## Slide 1: The Load Changes During the Climb

- The chain is specified as 0.15 kg/m.
- The hanging chain length changes as the vehicle rises, changing the load case.
- Size the drive for the worst credible condition, then validate it on the rig.

---

## Slide 2: Start With a Free-Body Diagram

- Gravity: $F_g = mg$.
- Drive force must overcome gravity, losses and acceleration demand.
- Roller torque: $T = Fr$.
- Mechanical power: $P = Fv$.

---

## Slide 3: Motor and Gearbox Selection

- Use measured or data-sheet torque-speed-current data.
- Check stall current against driver, fuse, wiring and battery capability.
- Include gearbox and contact efficiency; avoid choosing from no-load speed alone.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - Bench Characterisation

- Measure roller traction on the intended pipe surface.
- Record motor current and speed at defined loads.
- Produce a first drive sizing calculation and identify safety factor.

---

## Slide 5: Week 3 Evidence

- Free-body diagram and calculation sheet.
- Traction and motor test data.
- Justified motor, gearbox and roller selection.
