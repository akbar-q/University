# Week 7 - Full Mission Verification and Scrutineering

---

## Slide 1: Verify the Whole Mission

- Start at the defined datum position.
- Climb under representative chain load.
- Perform top dwell with required indications.
- Descend, release in the valid zone and complete the stop condition.

---

## Slide 2: Record Performance Properly

- Overall and net run time.
- Engagement duration.
- Chain-release position.
- Green/red/buzzer operation.
- Pass/fail observations, cause and corrective action.

---

## Slide 3: Scrutineering Readiness

- Safe wiring, fused battery, retained components and guarded motion.
- Vehicle remains within envelope; clip exception is documented.
- BoM includes the automatic clip, cost and mass.
- Staff sign-off material is complete before the event.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - Mock Heat

- Run three representative full missions.
- Treat each run as evidence, not merely a trial.
- Make only controlled changes between runs and record their effect.

---

## Slide 5: Week 7 Evidence

- Completed test plan and mission data.
- Compliance checklist and final BoM/cost/mass.
- Staff safety-sign-off draft and prioritised final improvements.
