## LO4 preparation: safety evidence plan + risk assessment structure

Session length: **4 hours**.

Targets (this session):
- Prepare students to succeed in **LO4 P8/M4/D4** by:
  - planning what safety systems evidence they will capture (P8)
  - planning what analysis they will write (M4)
  - scaffolding the safe working plan + full risk assessment (D4)

Evidence produced today:
- LO4 Safety Pack structure + evidence capture plan.
- Risk assessment template started (hazards/controls/residual risk).

## Slide 3 — What counts as “investigate” (P8)
**On-slide:**
- Identify safety features
- Explain purpose
- Evidence: photos/diagrams/screenshots (where permitted)

**Speaker notes (6–8 min):**
- Stress evidence: not just claims.

---

## Slide 4 — What counts as “analyse” (M4)
**On-slide:**
- Link hazard → safety feature → operating mode → user interface → safe behaviour
- Explain *how* the system reduces risk

**Speaker notes (8–10 min):**
- Model an analysis paragraph structure.

---

## Slide 5 — What counts as “design a safe working plan” (D4)
**On-slide:**
- Clear task definition
- Step-by-step method statement
- Stop conditions + emergency actions
- Roles/responsibilities

**Speaker notes (8–10 min):**
- Provide examples: “When to stop”, “How to isolate”.

---

## Slide 6 — Risk assessment structure (D4 backbone)
**On-slide:**
- Hazard
- Who is at risk
- Initial risk
- Control measures
- Residual risk
- Verification checks

**Speaker notes (10 min):**
- Explain “verification checks” as: how you confirm controls exist and are used.

---

## Slide 7 — Good hazards: cover the full lifecycle
