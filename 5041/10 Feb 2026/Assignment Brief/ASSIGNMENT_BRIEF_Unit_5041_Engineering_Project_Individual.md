# Task 1 — Project Proposal (LO1)

## What you must do
You will **propose** an engineering-based project brief and **develop** project aims and objectives. You will **undertake** a feasibility study to justify your proposal and **illustrate** how legislation, ethics and risk affect your project.

## Required content (use headings + evidence)
1) **Project shortlisting and selection**
- Identify at least **3** candidate project ideas.
- Develop **selection criteria** (e.g., time constraints, risk evaluation, cost, materials availability, skills/tools availability, user needs).
- **Select and justify** your final project choice using your criteria.

2) **Engineering problem definition (research-based)**
- Use **primary and/or secondary research** to explain the problem and context.
- Identify relevant **technical/industry standards**, **legislative constraints**, and **ethical considerations**.

3) **Project aims and objectives (SMART)**
- **Develop** clear project aims (what you intend to achieve).
- **Develop** SMART objectives (how you will achieve the aims).
- Briefly explain the **difference between aims and objectives** and show that yours are consistent.

4) **Feasibility study**
- **Undertake** a feasibility study covering:
  - constraints (time, cost, components, tools, access)
  - technical feasibility (modelling assumptions / sensor limits / computational limits)
  - safety feasibility (hazards, controls, safe scope)
- Conclude with a clear **go/no-go** decision and scope adjustments.

5) **Regulatory, ethical and risk factors**
- **Illustrate** how current legislation, ethics and risk influence your project choices.
- Include health and safety policy/procedure awareness and a simple risk assessment approach.

## Checklist of evidence required
- Proposal report section (or a standalone proposal document)
- Shortlisting table + selection criteria + justified selection
- Initial research summary (with Harvard references)
- Aims + objectives (SMART)
- Feasibility study (constraints + decision)
- Initial hazard/risk identification + controls

## Criteria covered by this task
- **LO1.P1** Propose an engineering-based project brief in line with national and international engineering regulatory and ethical frameworks.
- **LO1.P2** Develop engineering-based project proposal aims and objectives.
- **LO1.M1** Undertake a feasibility study to justify engineering-based project proposal.
- **LO1.D1** Illustrate the effects of relevant current legislation, ethics and risk in developing an engineering-based project proposal.

# Task 2 — Project Management Plan in Software (LO2)

## What you must do
You will **create** a project plan using project management software, tools and techniques. You will **make full use** of the software to develop a comprehensive plan, including **evaluation criteria**.

## Required content (use headings + evidence)
1) **Tool selection and justification**
- Identify at least **2** project management tools/software options (e.g., Jira, MS Project, Trello, Excel, Smartsheet).
- **Justify** your chosen tool against criteria such as usability, level of detail, flexibility, tracking, and evidence export.

2) **Comprehensive project plan (in software)**
Your plan must include:
- Work breakdown structure (WBS) translated into tasks/issues
- Timeline with milestones (e.g., Week 2 proposal baseline, Week 3 plan baseline, Week 6 results set, Week 8 presentation)
- Dependencies as **personal project dependencies** (learning prerequisites, lead times for parts, tool access)
- Risk register + change log + decision log
- Weekly status reporting approach (planned vs actual)

3) **Evaluation criteria (built into the plan)**
- Define measurable evaluation criteria (e.g., KPI table, decision matrix, acceptance criteria)
- Show how each objective is evaluated (test/simulation method + pass/fail threshold)

4) **Monitoring regime**
- Explain how you will update the plan weekly and how progress will be tracked (burndown, status categories, planned vs actual hours)

## Checklist of evidence required
- Screenshots/exports from your project management software showing:
  - backlog/WBS/tasks
  - milestones and schedule view
  - dependencies
  - risk register + change log + decision log
  - evaluation criteria/KPIs linked to objectives
- Short written justification of tool selection

# Task 3 — Implementation + Technical Engineering Report (LO3)

## What you must do
You will **implement** required project activities, **record** progress against the original plan, **produce** a coherent technical engineering project report, and **conduct or simulate** planned project activities to generate outcomes that solve the identified engineering problem. You will also **review** practical examples to solve potential structural or performance-based issues using simulation software or experimental approaches. Finally, you will **critically analyse** outcomes against the plan and make justified recommendations.

## Required content (use headings + evidence)
1) **Execution and monitoring**
- **Implement** planned activities and **record** progress (planned vs actual).
- Show evidence of monitoring (weekly status, variance, root cause analysis where needed).
- **Justify** any changes (scope/time/technical approach) and update the plan accordingly.

2) **Engineering method and build/simulation**
- Path A (MATLAB): modelling assumptions, model validation, controller/algorithm design, simulation runs, parameter sweeps/fault injection where relevant.
- Path B (hardware): system architecture, wiring/pin map (if applicable), firmware/software structure, integration steps, test harness or logging.

3) **Testing/validation and results**
- Define acceptance criteria linked to objectives.
- **Conduct or simulate** tests/simulations and present results with units and uncertainty/limitations where appropriate.
- Include at least one reliability/robustness angle (edge cases, faults, tolerances, noise, saturation, missing data).

4) **Practical examples and problem solving**
- **Review** at least **2** practical examples where you addressed a structural/performance issue using:
  - simulation, or
  - experimental testing
- Show before/after evidence (plots, test logs, results tables) and explain what changed.

5) **Technical engineering report**
Your report must be structured and include:
- introduction + background/research
- method/design
- implementation
- testing/validation
- project monitoring (plan vs actual)
- discussion + limitations
- recommendations for further work
- conclusion

## Checklist of evidence required
- Technical engineering report (main submission)
- Evidence pack (figures, plots, logs, photos/screenshots, test tables)
- Plan monitoring evidence (status reports, variance explanations, change log)

## Criteria covered by this task
- **LO3.P4** Implement required project activities, recording progress against original project plan.
- **LO3.P5** Produce a coherent technical engineering project report covering each stage of the project.
- **LO3.P6** Conduct or simulate planned project activities to generate outcomes which provide a solution to the identified engineering problem.
- **LO3.M3** Implement a full range of project activities, recording progress against original project plan.
- **LO3.M4** Produce a structured and detailed technical engineering project report covering each stage of the project that includes justified project outcomes.
- **LO3.M5** Review a range of practical examples to solve potential structural or performance-based issues using simulation software or experimental approaches.
- **LO3.D3** Critically analyse the project outcomes in the project report against the original project plan, making justified recommendations for further improvements.

# Task 4 — Presentation + Feedback + Reflection (LO4)

## What you must do
You will **present** your engineering-based project to a technically literate audience and **conduct** a feedback session. You will **reflect** on the project outcomes. You will **evaluate** end-to-end delivery in terms of your performance and how third-party feedback might be addressed. Finally, you will **critically evaluate** end-to-end delivery using reflective practice and **produce** an action plan and recommendations.

## Required content (use headings + evidence)
1) **Presentation (8–10 minutes + Q&A)**
Your presentation must include:
- project brief (problem, user need)
- aims/objectives + success criteria
- approach (plan, risks, changes)
- implementation overview (MATLAB model or hardware build)
- key results (tests/simulations) with a clear conclusion
- limitations + next steps

2) **Feedback session**
- **Conduct** a feedback/Q&A session.
- Record questions and feedback received.

3) **Reflection and action plan**
- **Reflect** on outcomes and your performance (what went well, what didn’t, what you learned).
- Use one reflective model (e.g., Gibbs, Kolb, Driscoll, 5R, Schön) and reference it.
- **Evaluate** how you delivered the project end-to-end and how feedback will be addressed.
- **Critically evaluate** your practice and **produce** an action plan (specific improvements + timescales).

## Checklist of evidence required
- Slide deck (PDF/ODP)
- Feedback record (questions + your responses + actions)
- Reflection + action plan document/section

## Criteria covered by this task
- **LO4.P7** Present the engineering-based project using appropriate media to a technically literate audience, and conduct a feedback session.
- **LO4.P8** Reflect on project outcomes.
- **LO4.M6** Evaluate the end-to-end delivery of the engineering-based project in terms of own performance and how third-party feedback might be addressed.
- **LO4.D4** Critically evaluate the end-to-end delivery of the engineering-based project in terms of reflective practice and produce an action plan and recommendations for how third-party feedback might be addressed.
