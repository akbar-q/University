# LO4 Professional Discussion (PD) — 10–15 min Examiner Sheet + Question Bank

Purpose:
- Enable a consistent 10–15 minute PD per student.
- Provide question variety while still covering Pass/Merit/Distinction depth.

Use:
- Pick 1 question from Section A (Pass), 1–2 from Section B (Merit), 1 from Section C (Distinction).
- Use follow-ups to probe for evidence and critical thinking.

---

## Timing plan (suggested)
- 0:00–1:00 — Student intro (role + subsystem)
- 1:00–6:00 — Pass coverage (what they did + evidence)
- 6:00–11:00 — Merit coverage (evaluation + responding to feedback/changes)
- 11:00–15:00 — Distinction probe (critical judgement, trade-offs, improvements)

If running 10 minutes:
- 0:00–1:00 intro
- 1:00–5:00 pass
- 5:00–8:00 merit
- 8:00–10:00 distinction probe

---

## Evidence prompts (use these as follow-ups)
Ask for at least one evidence item per key claim:
- Jira issue keys owned, screenshots, or exports
- Serial logs with timestamps
- Test case IDs + results table
- Photos/videos of build and demo
- Change/decision log entries

---

## Section A — Pass-level questions (P7/P8-aligned coverage)
Pick at least 1.

1) Role and contribution
- Q: “What was your subsystem responsibility and what did you personally deliver?”
- Follow-ups:
  - “Which Jira issues show this?”
  - “What evidence proves your work functions?”

2) System explanation
- Q: “Explain how the full system works end-to-end (inputs → processing → outputs).”
- Follow-ups:
  - “What are the main states and transitions?”
  - “What is the safest failure mode?”

3) Requirements/KPIs
- Q: “Which objective/KPI mattered most and how did you measure it?”
- Follow-ups:
  - “What was the target and what did you actually achieve?”
  - “How many trials did you run?”

4) Testing basics
- Q: “Describe one integration test you ran and what it proved.”
- Follow-ups:
  - “What was the expected behaviour? What was observed?”

---

## Section B — Merit-level questions (deeper evaluation + responding)
Pick 1–2.

1) Managing feedback (presentation/demo feedback)
- Q: “What feedback did you receive and what did you change because of it?”
- Follow-ups:
  - “Show where you recorded the feedback and the action you took.”
  - “What improved (measured) after the change?”

2) Planning vs actual (monitoring + adjustments)
- Q: “Where did the project deviate from the plan, and how did you respond?”
- Follow-ups:
  - “What did Jira/monitoring reveal?”
  - “What was the root cause and your mitigation?”

3) Dependency management
- Q: “Describe a dependency that blocked you (or that you blocked for others) and how you handled it.”
- Follow-ups:
  - “What did you do to unblock early (stub/mock/ICD publish)?”

4) Issue resolution (practical)
- Q: “Give a practical example of a performance/structural issue and how you solved it.”
- Follow-ups:
  - “What evidence do you have (before/after)?”
  - “How did it affect a KPI?”

---

## Section C — Distinction probe (critical, justified judgement)
Pick at least 1.

1) Trade-offs and engineering judgement
- Q: “What was the biggest trade-off you made and why was it the right decision?”
- Follow-ups:
  - “What did you lose by not choosing the alternative?”
  - “How did you mitigate the downside?”

2) Critical evaluation of outcomes
- Q: “Critically evaluate your outcomes against the original plan and objectives.”
- Follow-ups:
  - “Which variance mattered most and why?”
  - “What evidence supports your conclusion?”

3) Improvement plan (justified)
- Q: “Propose three improvements: one technical, one testing/quality, one project/process. Justify each.”
- Follow-ups:
  - “Which KPI would improve and how much do you expect?”
  - “What effort/cost/risk is involved?”

4) Robustness and safety
- Q: “If this were used by a real user, what’s the most dangerous failure mode and how would you reduce it?”
- Follow-ups:
  - “How would you prove it is safer (test/verification)?”

---

## Marking consistency prompts (quick rubric-style)
Use these checks while listening:
- Pass: clear explanation + evidence + accurate system understanding
- Merit: responds to feedback/monitoring, evaluates and justifies adjustments with evidence
- Distinction: critical analysis, trade-offs, justified improvements, quantified where possible
