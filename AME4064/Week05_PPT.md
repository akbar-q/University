# Week 5 - Batteries and Motor Drives

---

## Slide 1: Battery Language

- Voltage: potential supplied by the pack.
- Capacity: charge available, commonly Ah.
- Energy estimate: $E \approx V \times Ah$.
- C-rate and internal resistance limit the current a pack can supply safely.

---

## Slide 2: Series and Parallel Cells

- Series increases voltage; capacity in Ah is unchanged.
- Parallel increases capacity/current capability; voltage is unchanged.
- Only combine matched cells using an appropriate protected holder or pack.

---

## Slide 3: Motor Drivers

- An H-bridge controls forward, reverse, coast and brake states.
- Choose current rating against stall current, not nominal running current.
- Fuse close to the battery; add a master switch and strain relief.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - Safe Drive System

- Wire battery or bench supply to fuse, switch, driver and motor.
- Verify polarity before power-on.
- Demonstrate forward, reverse and stop states.

---

## Slide 5: Weekly Output

- Power-path schematic.
- Runtime estimate for a stated average current.
- Pre-power safety checklist.
