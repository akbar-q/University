# Week 4 - Motors, Gearboxes and Motion

---

## Slide 1: What a Motor Data Sheet Tells Us

- Nominal voltage and no-load speed.
- No-load current and stall current.
- Stall torque and torque-speed behaviour.
- These values determine driver, fuse, battery and gearbox choices.

---

## Slide 2: Gearboxes Exchange Speed for Torque

- Ideal gearbox relation: $T_{out} = G T_{motor}$.
- Ideal speed relation: $\omega_{out} = \omega_{motor}/G$.
- Real systems lose energy through friction and inefficiency.
- Choose enough torque margin before selecting a fast gear ratio.

---

## Slide 3: From Rotation to Linear Travel

- Surface speed: $v = r\omega$.
- Traction must overcome load and resistance without slipping.
- Contact material, normal force and roller geometry matter.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - Characterise a Geared Motor

- Measure no-load speed and current.
- Apply a controlled load and record changed speed/current.
- Calculate roller speed from diameter and compare against measurement.

---

## Slide 5: Weekly Output

- Motor selection table.
- Speed and current plot.
- One design recommendation based on measured data.
