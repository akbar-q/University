# Week 7 - Traction, Power and Prototype Integration

---

## Slide 1: Turning a Requirement Into Numbers

- Define the load, distance, speed and duty cycle.
- Estimate force from gravity and resistance.
- Convert required force to wheel/roller torque: $T = Fr$.
- Estimate mechanical power: $P = Fv$.

---

## Slide 2: Traction Is a Design Requirement

- Available friction: $F_{traction} \leq \mu N$.
- More normal force may improve grip but can raise losses and jam risk.
- Test real materials on real surfaces; do not design from a catalogue alone.

---

## Slide 3: Prototype Requirement

- Start from a defined position.
- Move a stated load or distance.
- Stop at a switch or marker.
- Remain safe, inspectable and within the agreed cost limit.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - Pair Build

- Integrate chassis, drive, power and control.
- Use staged tests: electrical, unloaded motion, loaded motion, stop behaviour.
- Record every failure and correction in the engineering log.

---

## Slide 5: Weekly Output

- Requirements list and initial BoM.
- Force, torque and power calculation.
- Risk assessment before the first moving test.
