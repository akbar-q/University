# Week 1 - Introduction to Mechatronics

---

## Slide 1: From Mechanical System to Mechatronic System

- A mechatronic product combines structure, energy, sensing, control and motion.
- Challenge examples: line-following vehicle, repeatable car and pipe climber.
- Today: identify the energy path and information path in a simple vehicle.

Speaker notes: Mechanical design still matters; the electronics make the mechanical system observable and controllable.

---

## Slide 2: Essential Electrical Quantities

- Voltage $V$: electrical potential difference, measured in volts.
- Current $I$: charge flow, measured in amperes.
- Resistance $R$: opposition to current, measured in ohms.
- Power $P = VI$: rate of energy transfer, measured in watts.

---

## Slide 3: A Vehicle Block Diagram

`Battery -> fuse/switch -> motor driver -> motor/gearbox -> wheel or roller`

`Switch/sensor -> controller -> motor driver`

- The controller does not power the motor directly.
- A safe design gives every component a named purpose.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - Measure Before Building

- Identify multimeter terminals and select the correct measurement mode.
- Measure supply voltage and resistor value.
- Build an LED circuit with a current-limiting resistor.
- Check polarity before energising any circuit.

---

## Slide 5: Exit Evidence

- Draw a block diagram for a small autonomous vehicle.
- Label its power and signal paths.
- Calculate $P$ for a 6 V load drawing 0.20 A.
- State one hazard and one control.
