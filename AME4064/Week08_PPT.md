# Week 8 - Test, Improve and Communicate

---

## Slide 1: A Demonstration Is Not a Test Plan

- Define objective, method, inputs, expected output and pass criterion.
- Run more than once to assess repeatability.
- Record actual values, units and observed failure modes.

---

## Slide 2: Useful Evaluation Questions

- Did the prototype meet its stated requirements?
- Where is the largest uncertainty or risk?
- Which design change has the greatest expected benefit?
- What evidence supports that decision?

---

## Slide 3: Three-Minute Technical Briefing

- Problem and requirements.
- System diagram and one key calculation.
- Test result and design improvement.
- Cost, safety and what the team learned.

---

## Slide 4: Practical - Demonstrate and Review

- Run the agreed test sequence.
- Conduct peer observation without changing another group's build.
- Complete the individual competency check: safe measurement, correct wiring explanation and fault response.

---

## Slide 5: Final Evidence

- Engineering log, BoM and risk record.
- Test plan and results.
- Evaluation and action list.
- Technical briefing slides.
