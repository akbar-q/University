# 02 AI Models and Prompting - Day 1

This folder is a **multi-day casual training module** on:
- how modern AI language models work (engineering mental model)
- how training/alignment shapes behavior
- how to prompt reliably (structured prompts + verification loops)
- applied workflows (engineering tasks)

## Recommended schedule
- Day 1 (60–90 min): What AI models are doing (tokens, inference loop, transformers)
- Day 2 (60–90 min): Training + alignment + hallucinations
- Day 3 (60–90 min): Prompting patterns (templates, grounding, structured output)
- Day 4 (60–90 min): Applied workflows + mini-assessment (optional)
